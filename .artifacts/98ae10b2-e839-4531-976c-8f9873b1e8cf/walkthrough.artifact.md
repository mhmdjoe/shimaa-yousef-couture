# Walkthrough: Social Media Integration

I have successfully added interactive, editable social media icons to your website's footer. This allows your clients to connect with your brand on Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest.

## Changes Made

### 🌐 Social Media Connectivity
- **Interactive Icons**: Added elegant icons for **Instagram**, **Facebook**, and **Pinterest** in the footer.
- **Dynamic Linking**: These icons are functional and will open your social media profiles in a new browser tab when clicked.

### 🛡️ Admin Social Control
- **Edit on the Fly**: Just like the text and photos, these social links are now fully manageable in Admin Mode.
- **Easy Updates**: In Admin Mode (`admin123`), clicking any social icon opens a dialog where you can paste your profile link and click **SAVE CHANGES**.

### 🛠️ Technical Improvements
- **URL Launcher**: Integrated the `url_launcher` package to safely handle external navigation across all devices.
- **Unified Aesthetic**: The icons are styled in Gold/White to perfectly match the Shimaa Yousef Couture design language.

## How to Set Your Social Links
1.  **Log in** as Admin (`admin123`).
2.  Scroll to the **Footer** at the bottom of any page.
3.  **Click a Social Icon** (e.g., the Instagram camera).
4.  Paste your full profile link (like `https://www.instagram.com/shimaayousef/`).
5.  Click **SAVE CHANGES**.
6.  Logout to test the link as a client!

> [!TIP]
> Make sure to include the `https://` part of the link so it works correctly on all devices.

> [!NOTE]
> These links are saved in your browser's memory, so they will persist across sessions on your device.
